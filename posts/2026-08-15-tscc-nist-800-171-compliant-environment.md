The San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC) has launched a NIST SP 800-171 compliant environment
on the Triton Shared Computing Cluster (TSCC), providing a secure platform for researchers
handling sensitive datasets, such as NIH Controlled Access Data (CAD). This new capability ensures
researchers can securely process regulated biomedical and genomic data while meeting stringent
federal cybersecurity and data protection requirements.

## Who should use this environment

Researchers who work with CAD or other sensitive research data requiring NIST SP 800-171 compliance
should request access to the TSCC compliant environment. Accessing NIH CAD requires an institutional
attestation confirming the environment meets these security requirements. Researchers should
coordinate with their campus Office of Information Assurance or Health Sciences IT organization to
obtain this attestation.

## Available usage models

The TSCC compliant environment maintains the flexibility expected from a shared research computing
system, offering two allocation models:

* **Hotel Program**: Ideal for short-term or variable usage, offering flexible, pay-as-you-go
  compute access.
* **Condo Program**: Allows principal investigators to purchase dedicated nodes within the shared
  cluster for their research.

Both models are centrally managed by the TSCC team and are fully available within the secure
environment.

## Impact for researchers

Achieving NIST SP 800-171 compliance involved a rigorous review of system security, controls, and
data handling practices. By providing this environment, SDSC streamlines access to advanced
computing resources. Researchers can leverage the performance of TSCC without compromising their
security or compliance obligations.

For more details on access procedures, required documentation, and rates, please visit the
[TSCC NIST compliance information page](https://www.sdsc.edu/systems/tscc/nist.html).
