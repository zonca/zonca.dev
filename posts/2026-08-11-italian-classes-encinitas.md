My wife Maura D'Andrea is the School Director of the Italian school of San Diego.

Starting in September 2026, the school is opening a second in-person location at San Dieguito United Methodist Church, 170 Calle Magdalena in Encinitas.

The new North County programs include:

- Italian classes for children in TK through 5th grade on Mondays from 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM. Two small groups are organized by age, TK-2nd grade and 3rd-5th grade. No previous knowledge of Italian is required.
- Beginner and intermediate Italian classes for adults on Fridays from 6:00 PM to 7:30 PM.

All classes are taught by native Italian speakers.

See the complete schedule and enrollment information for [Italian classes in Encinitas](https://www.italianschoolsd.com/italian-classes-encinitas/), including the dedicated page for [adult Italian classes in Encinitas](https://www.italianschoolsd.com/news/2026/08/adult-italian-classes-encinitas-north-county-fall-2026/).
