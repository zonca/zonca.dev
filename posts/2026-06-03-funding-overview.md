I've been transparent about research funding for a while now, and here's an update on where my time goes across projects, institutions, and grants.

## The Numbers

My research time is split across multiple funding sources:

| Source | Allocation | Focus |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| **Indiana University** | 20% | Jetstream2, Kubernetes, JupyterHub |
| **UC Denver** | 49% | HPC, AI workloads, Voyager |
| **Berkeley** | Variable | TMT/IRIS, Cybershuttle |
| **PI Funding** | 3-8% | Independent research |
| **Expanse** | 10-28% | ACCESS allocation |
| **Cybershuttle** | 10-18% | Cloud infrastructure |

## What This Means

Each allocation supports specific research directions:

- **Indiana University** funds the Jetstream2 cloud platform work — Kubernetes deployments, JupyterHub configuration, and scalable computing infrastructure
- **UC Denver** supports deep learning workloads, particularly Voyager experiments with Intel Gaudi2 accelerators
- **Berkeley** covers TMT/IRIS collaborations and the Cybershuttle infrastructure
- **Expanse** (ACCESS) enables HPC research with NVIDIA GPU access
- **Cybershuttle** funds cloud-based computing platforms

## Transparency

I believe in being open about where research funding comes from and what it supports. This transparency helps:

1. Researchers understand funding landscapes
2. Institutions see ROI on their investments
3. The public tracks how research dollars are spent
