Request an HPC@UC allocation on Expanse at SDSC

How UC researchers can request a 500K core-hour allocation on SDSC’s Expanse supercomputer through the HPC@UC program, including eligibility, the application process, and trial accounts.

If you are a researcher at any University of California campus and need more computing power, the HPC@UC program at the San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC) gives you an accelerated and simple path to a substantial allocation on the Expanse supercomputer. This post explains what HPC@UC offers, who is eligible, and how to apply in 2026.

What is HPC@UC?

HPC@UC is a partnership between SDSC, the University of California Vice Chancellors of Research, and campus Chief Information Officers. It is designed to:

  • Broaden the base of UC researchers who use advanced computing
  • Provide an on-ramp for UC researchers who are new to high-performance computing
  • Seed promising computational research
  • Facilitate collaborations between SDSC and UC researchers
  • Help UC researchers be successful when applying for larger allocations through the NSF’s ACCESS program

Resources available

A successful request gives you access to SDSC’s HPC infrastructure and expertise:

  • Expanse: SDSC’s flagship 5+ PFlop/s system, with 728 CPU nodes (two 64-core AMD EPYC 7742 processors and 256 GB memory per node), 4 large-memory nodes with 2 TB memory, and 52 GPU nodes. See the Expanse page for details.
  • Data storage: more than 12 PB of fast Lustre parallel storage, including short-term Performance Storage and long-term Project Storage, plus a Durable Storage copy of Project Storage data.
  • Software: a large installed base of HPC and big-data analytics applications.
  • Expertise: SDSC staff to help you make the best use of the resources and prepare larger allocation requests.

Allocation details

  • Size: up to 500K core-hours of computing, plus associated data storage and access to SDSC expertise.
  • Duration: awards are active for one year.
  • Review: applications are reviewed on an ongoing basis; you are notified within 10 business days of the review decision.

Eligibility

  • The program is intended for UC researchers who are new to high-performance computing.
  • You must not have an active award in the NSF’s ACCESS program.
  • You must not have previously received an HPC@UC award on Expanse.

How to apply

  1. Create an ACCESS account at https://identity.access-ci.org/new-user. Eligibility is evaluated through the ACCESS review infrastructure (XRAS), so you need an ACCESS_CI portal account first, as confirmed by the HPC@UC program staff.
  2. Contact HPC@UC at hpc_uc@sdsc.edu, or via the HPC@UC page, and submit a request/help ticket describing your research project so staff can evaluate your eligibility.
  3. Submit your request: once approved and an opportunity is opened, the ticket is updated with the instructions for submitting your allocation request, including a template to fill in.

Try Expanse first

If you are not sure Expanse is the right fit, request a Trial Account on Expanse before applying for HPC@UC: 1,000 core-hours, typically fulfilled within 1 working day, designed for a quick assessment of the system. You are still eligible for an HPC@UC allocation even if you already have a Trial Account. See the Trial Account section of the Expanse User Guide.

Related options at SDSC

  • TSCC (Triton Shared Computing Cluster): a paid cluster at SDSC; UCSD researchers currently benefit from subsidized costs. See the TSCC page.
  • ACCESS allocations: for larger, longer-term needs you can apply directly to the ACCESS program. HPC@UC is intended as a stepping stone toward these larger national allocations.
